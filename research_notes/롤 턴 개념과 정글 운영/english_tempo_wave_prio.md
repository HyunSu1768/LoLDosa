## 1. How coaches define tempo, and how it is gained or lost

### Takeaway
English-language coaching mostly defines "tempo" as **free time**: the time you have left over, after your "duties" (clearing the wave, recalling), to act before your opponent can respond. You gain it by finishing your wave first, ideally crashing it and then recalling. You lose it by recalling or roaming while the wave is pushing toward you, or by resetting at bad moments. This is the closest English match to the Korean '턴' ("having the turn").

### Cited Findings
- LoL Theory: "think of wave management as tempo — net free time after your 'duties' (clearing minions, recalling)." The same guide: "Fast pushing creates tempo by finishing your duty quickly. Freezing trades your tempo for denying your opponent's. Slow pushing banks tempo for a future crash." The guide attributes this framing to "high-elo coaching" but names no coach. — [LoL Theory blog](https://blog.loltheory.gg/wave-management-league-of-legends/)
- Boosteria defines tempo as the ability to act before the enemy can respond. With tempo you can take vision, start objectives, or invade while the opponent is stuck catching a wave or finishing a recall. It also says recalling on a wave that is still pushing toward you costs farm and gives the enemy tempo. — [Boosteria macro guide](https://boosteria.org/guides/understanding-macro-play-basics-league)
- NerfPlz: the usual way to get tempo in lane is to finish the wave before your opponent. The crashed wave gives you a few seconds of free time for a camp, ward, roam, or recall. — [NerfPlz, "Tempo Explained" (2026)](https://www.nerfplz.com/2026/09/tempo-explained-what-it-means-and-how.html)
- LoL Theory: the best recall timing is right after a slow-pushed wave crashes into the enemy tower, because you lose little CS and the tower resets the wave. — [LoL Theory blog](https://blog.loltheory.gg/wave-management-league-of-legends/)
- Jungle tempo, per Boosteria: chaining efficient actions (clearing camps smoothly, arriving at fights first, resetting at good moments). It lists bad resets, such as recalling while your side's camps are up, as a common way to lose tempo. — [Boosteria jungle pathing guide](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-pathing-basics-tempo-tracking-gank-windows)
- Boosteria's jungle pathing guide also has "tempo" in its title, alongside tracking and gank windows. — [Boosteria jungle pathing guide](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-pathing-basics-tempo-tracking-gank-windows)

### Inferences
- "Tempo" in English is close to the Korean '턴' in meaning. "Having tempo" ≈ '턴을 가졌다', and "losing tempo" or "giving the enemy tempo" ≈ '턴을 넘겨줬다'. "Free time" / "free turn" describes the resource itself. A crash followed by a recall is the typical way to create it.
- One asymmetry follows from these definitions. Whoever crashes first can recall, roam, or ward for free. The opponent's time is then spent catching the wave, so they cannot answer.

## 2. Wave states and the free time each one gives

### Takeaway
The standard vocabulary is **slow push, fast push (hard shove), freeze, crash, bounce, and cannon wave**. A crash, whether from a fast push or a slow push, creates free time: the wave resets at the enemy tower and bounces back. A freeze holds the wave near your own tower. You give up mobility to deny the opponent farm and safety, which drains their time. A half-finished push hands the opponent a freeze.

### Cited Findings
- **Slow push**: you kill only the enemy's caster minions and last-hit your own, so your wave grows as it walks toward their tower. "The slow-push develops over 3 waves typically." — [search snippets from Mobalytics / LoL Theory / mid.gg wave guides](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/guides/wave-management); also [mid.gg](https://mid.gg/guides/wave-management)
- One guide says a slow push "typically takes over two or three waves to snowball". Another describes the ideal setup as "slow push over 2–3 waves, crash the resulting large stack, then recall immediately." — [NerfPlz wave management (2026)](https://www.nerfplz.com/2026/08/wave-management-explained-freezing-slow.html); [Buildzcrank 2026](https://buildzcrank.com/en/blog/wave-management-complete-guide-lol/)
- **Fast push**: "The goal is to shove it under the enemy tower, buying yourself free time." — [LoL Theory blog](https://blog.loltheory.gg/wave-management-league-of-legends/)
- **Crash**: "Crash the wave into the enemy tower before every recall, roam or objective." — [Boosting Market wave guide](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-wave-management-guide/) (from the search snippet)
- **Bounce**: "After a large wave crashes into the enemy tower, the wave will naturally push back toward your side." Some guides call the bounce one of the best moments to set a freeze or to start a slow push for the next crash. — [Manabuy advanced wave guide](https://manabuy.com/blog/news/advanced-wave-management-guide-freezing-bouncing-league-legends); [mid.gg](https://mid.gg/guides/wave-management)
- **The bounce limits free time**: if you detour through the jungle or take a long walk, "that bounce reaches your tower before you do and the free back stops being free." — [mid.gg recall timing](https://mid.gg/guides/recall-timing) / [Buildzcrank](https://buildzcrank.com/en/blog/wave-management-complete-guide-lol/) (from the search snippet; the search tool did not say which of the two pages the quote came from)
- **Freeze**: you hold the wave near your own tower. "Freezing is one of the more complicated types of wave management to perfect." — [Manabuy](https://manabuy.com/blog/news/advanced-wave-management-guide-freezing-bouncing-league-legends)
- **Partial shove warning**: "A partial shove can leave the opponent with a freeze, so avoid starting one unless you can finish the crash." — [Boosting Market](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-wave-management-guide/) (from the search snippet)
- **Cannon wave**: "Every third wave includes a cannon minion"; "Never recall on a cannon wave unless forced to." — [Boosting Market](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-wave-management-guide/) / [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/guides/wave-management) (from search snippets)
- **Cheater recall** (uses the cannon-wave crash to create a back window): "hard-shove the third wave, the first cannon wave, into the enemy tower. Immediately after this wave crashes, you initiate your recall. The cannon minion takes longer for the tower to chew through, and the subsequent wave (the fourth) will meet the returning enemy minions further up the lane, and hence, you create a window." It also gives "the wave will crash into your opponent's tower at roughly 3 min and you should have ~450-500g". **These numbers predate patch 26.1**, when waves still spawned at 1:05. — [WeCoach cheater recall](https://wecoach.gg/blog/article/how-to-cheater-recall-in-league-of-legends); [Mobalytics top cheater recall](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-top-lane-wave-management-guide-cheater-recall/); [1v9.gg](https://1v9.gg/blog/how-to-cheater-recall-lol)
- **Wave travel time**: "Because the middle lane is shorter in length, minions arriving will only take 22 seconds to reach the middle of the lane instead of the 32 seconds in the side lanes." Old timing: "The first siege minion will spawn in at 2:05, and get to lane at around 2:37 in the side lanes and 2:27 in the mid lane." This is also pre-26.1. — [Mobalytics wave management guide](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/wave-management/) (from the search snippet)

### Inferences
- How much free time each wave state gives (my synthesis of the sources above):
  - **Crash, after a fast or slow push**: the most free time, for recall, roam, ward, scuttle, or invade. The length is roughly the time the enemy tower needs to kill your wave plus the time the next waves need to meet and bounce back. Bigger crashes and cannon crashes give longer windows.
  - **Bounce**: your free time ends when the bounced wave arrives at your tower. You have to be back before then, or you lose minions or tower HP.
  - **Freeze**: no free time for the freezer, who is pinned to the lane. The opponent loses time too, because they cannot farm safely and will be overextended if they try.
  - **Wave pushing toward you / uncrashed**: no free time. Leaving now loses CS and gives the opponent tempo, i.e. '턴을 내줌'.
- Travel-time arithmetic under the 26.1 spawn timing (my calculation, not from any source): first spawn 0:30 plus 22 s (mid) or 32 s (side) puts the first meeting at about 0:52 in mid and 1:02 in side lanes. The first siege wave spawns at 1:30, so it would reach mid lane around 1:52 and the side lanes around 2:02. This assumes travel times did not change in 26.1.

- No source gives a precise number for how many seconds of free time a given crash produces. Neither the wiki nor any coaching site in the results gave a "bounce timer".

## 3. Lane priority (prio) and why it matters for objectives and the jungle

### Takeaway
"Prio" means your wave is pushed toward the enemy, so your laner can move first and the opponent has to stay and catch the wave. Junglers spend their laners' prio. They fight and take objectives next to pushed lanes, and above all they want mid and bot prio before Dragon spawns. Starting an objective without prio risks the jungler being collapsed on.

### Cited Findings
- Prio is when "your minion wave is pushed toward the enemy tower, so your opponent has to stay and farm while you're free to move first." — [MOBA Champion, "Lane Priority in LoL Explained"](https://www.mobachampion.com/blog/lane-priority-in-lol/)
- Junglers use their laners' prio by fighting and taking objectives near pushed lanes. — [Proview glossary](https://proview.es/en/glossary/prioridad-de-linea) / [MOBA Champion](https://www.mobachampion.com/blog/lane-priority-in-lol/)
- "the best time to get lane priority is before objectives spawn", because your team wants to take the objective right away. — [Agatasmurf, "Prio in League"](https://agatasmurf.com/prio-league-of-legends/)
- Starting an objective without priority "can leave the jungler alone against two opponents who arrive first." — [MOBA Champion](https://www.mobachampion.com/blog/lane-priority-in-lol/) (from the search snippet)
- "if the enemy jungler ganks the lane often, it's best to forfeit attempting to gain priority over the lane." — [MOBA Champion](https://www.mobachampion.com/blog/lane-priority-in-lol/)
- Mid sits between both sides of the map, so **mid-jungle prio** often decides who controls objectives. The setup can backfire on a misjudged dive: "giving a double or triple kill to the enemy top-laner or the bot lane can be disastrous." — [Sportskeeda, "What is mid-jungle prio"](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/what-mid-jungle-prio-professional-league-legends)
- Note on age: some prio sources date from Season 10–13. The definition of prio is stable, but jungle specifics may differ today.

### Inferences
- Prio is the lane-level form of tempo. The team with prio in the lanes next to an objective effectively "has the turn" ('턴') for that objective. A jungler deciding whether to start Dragon should first check whether mid and bot have prio.

## 4. Syncing backs / recall timing between laners and the jungler

### Takeaway
The consensus is: crash the wave, recall, buy your power spike, and return before the next wave reaches your tower. Ping your back timing early so the jungler can recall at the same time ("sync"). Then the whole side of the map returns together with fresh items for the next objective or skirmish. Junglers time their recalls around camp respawns and objective spawns rather than item thresholds alone.

### Cited Findings
- Mid.gg calls crash-then-back the default for every lane and recommends pinging your back timing early so the jungler can sync. — [mid.gg recall timing](https://mid.gg/guides/recall-timing)
- A clean reset means crashing the wave, recalling, buying your spike, and returning before the next wave arrives. — [Boosting Market recall guide](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-recall-timing-guide/)
- Avoid recalling while the wave is heading toward your turret, since the minions can die under it or damage it. — [LoL Alchemy Lab](https://lolalchemylab.com/en/guias/cuando-volver-a-base/)
- Statup.gg ties a second cannon-wave recall to the jungler's raptor clear and the roughly 3:30 Scuttle timing, so the two can coordinate vision or plays. The article is about 424 days old (2025), so check its timings against 26.1. — [Statup.gg](https://statup.gg/post/perfect-recall-timing-in-league-of-legends)
- Junglers should reset before important camps or objectives spawn, rather than recalling for item thresholds the way a mid laner would. The article is more than 1,000 days old. — [LeagueFeed](https://leaguefeed.net/how-to-efficiently-use-recall-in-league-of-legends/)
- NerfPlz calls backing while dead "the worst and most common" recall. — [NerfPlz (2026)](https://www.nerfplz.com/2026/10/losing-your-lane-in-lol-how-to-get-back.html)

### Inferences
- "Synced backs" turn individual free time into team tempo. If the mid laner and jungler crash and recall together, they come back with items at the same time and can contest the next objective or invade as a 2v1 or 2v2. A desync means one of them comes back alone, which gives up the 턴.

### Gaps
- Statup.gg's "17s rule" could not be verified; its contents did not appear in search snippets.

## 5. 'Free turn'/'free time' and trading objectives (cross-map trades)

### Takeaway
"Free time" is the standard English word for what a crash buys you. "Trading objectives" / "cross-map trading" means giving up one objective to take something guaranteed elsewhere, e.g. Herald or a top tower for Dragon. It is the right call when you can't win the contest and the return is certain.

### Cited Findings
- "Cross-map trade: giving one objective to take something elsewhere (tower, camps, another objective)." — [Boosteria macro basics](https://boosteria.org/guides/league-macro-basics-roams-lane-swaps-objectives) / [Boostroom 2026](https://boostroom.com/blog/objective-control-guide-dragons-herald-baron-and-setup-basics) (from search snippets)
- "If the enemy sends 4 bot for Dragon and you can't contest, you can take Herald/top tower instead." "Trading is correct when your cross-map play is guaranteed value (tower, plates, jungle camps, or a safer objective)." "If taking it costs a dragon you needed or forces a losing fight, trade instead." — [Buildzcrank macro guide 2026](https://buildzcrank.com/en/blog/macro-guide-league-of-legends-2026/); [Boosteria objective priorities](https://boosteria.org/guides/comprehensive-guide-objective-management-in-league) (from search snippets; the search tool did not say which quote came from which page)
- "Dragon for Herald + plates" is listed as a valid trade. "Herald: tempo tool to break towers, especially mid (map unlock)." Herald spawn was given as 8:00, takeable until 19:45. — [Boostroom 2026](https://boostroom.com/blog/objective-control-guide-dragons-herald-baron-and-setup-basics); [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/comprehensive-guide-objective-management-in-league) (Herald timings come from a third-party snippet; check them against the current patch)

### Inferences
- A cross-map trade is a way to "spend" the 턴 you have, or to salvage value when the enemy holds it. A team that has prio on one side and lacks it on the other should take what its prio side allows and concede the other side.

## 6. Game constants (current season, patch 26.1 / Season 2026)

### Takeaway
**Patch 26.1 (January 2026) changed the classic constants significantly.** First wave spawn moved from 1:05 to **0:30**. Waves spawn every **30 s** until 14:00, every **25 s** after 14:00, and every **20 s** after 30:00. The first siege (cannon) minion arrives with wave 3, at **1:30**. Recall is **8 s**; empowered recall (Baron or Herald-related buffs) cuts it to about 4 s. Most coaching guides still quote the old 1:05 / 2:05 numbers.

### Cited Findings
- 26.1: "First wave spawn time reduced to 0:30 from 1:05." "After 14:00, minion waves now spawn every 25 seconds. Waves that feature a siege minion will also lack one melee minion." After 30:00, minion waves spawn every 20 seconds, and siege waves "will lack one melee minion and one caster minion." — [Riot Patch 26.1 Notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/); [LoL Wiki V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01); [Sheep Esports 26.1](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/patch-26-1-new-role-quests-atakhan-and-feats-of-strength-removed-new-items-added/en); [Riftpatchnotes minions](https://www.riftpatchnotes.com/lol/system/minions) (all via search snippets)
- Riot's stated rationale (from the snippet): more windows for successful pushing. The extra gold and XP is offset by removing an occasional melee minion mid-game and lowering the value of super and cannon minions. Also listed: minion-to-turret damage up from 55% to 60%. — [Riot Patch 26.1 Notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/) (via search snippet)
- One source said the old first spawn was 1:30. **This conflicts** with the 1:05 that Riot's notes give; 1:05 is the long-standing value. — [Sportskeeda 26.1](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/league-legends-patch-26-1-notes) vs. [Riot notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/)
- First siege minion: the LoL Wiki lists the first siege wave as wave 3, at 01:30. — [LoL Wiki Minion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion_(League_of_Legends)) (via search snippet). One snippet also says "The first siege minion moved up to wave 3 from wave 4". That appears to be a historical patch-history line, and its patch is unclear.
- Siege cadence by game time: "1 Siege minion is spawned every three minion waves" for the first 15 minutes, every 2 waves from 15:05 to 25:00, and every wave from 25:00 on. — [Leaguepedia Siege Minion](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Siege_Minion); [LoL Fandom Siege minion](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/wiki/Siege_minion). **Possible conflict**: 26.1 changed the wave interval at 14:00, and one search summary said the "current wiki and patch notes use 14:00" for the breakpoint. I could not confirm whether the every-2-waves cannon cadence also moved to 14:00.
- Minion upgrades: "Minions 'upgrade' strictly every 90 seconds (i.e., every third wave)." In 26.1, at wave 15+, siege minion AD per upgrade is +4 instead of +1.5. — [LoL Wiki Minion](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minion_(League_of_Legends)); [V26.01](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V26.01) (via snippets). Note: after 14:00 waves come every 25 s, so "90 s = every third wave" may no longer hold late in the game.
- Recall: "8 seconds (7 with Improved Recall)". The channel must complete to teleport. — [LoL Fandom (zh) Channel](https://leagueoflegends.fandom.com/zh/wiki/%E5%B0%8F%E5%85%B5?oldid=7304) / [LoL Wiki Recall](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Recall) (via search; the English wiki's own snippet omitted the time). The 8 s base has been stable for years. "Improved Recall" (7 s) was an old summoner mastery and is not current.
- Empowered Recall: Leaguepedia describes the Eye of the Herald version as cutting Recall by 4 s, to about 4 s. Hand of Baron also halves the channel time. — [Leaguepedia Eye of the Herald](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_the_Herald); [LoL Wiki Hand of Baron](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Hand_of_Baron)
- Wave travel: about 22 s to the middle of mid lane and about 32 s to the middle of side lanes. — [Mobalytics wave management](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/wave-management/) (via snippet; written before 26.1)

### Inferences
- Constants table for patch 26.x (derived; check in-client):
  - Wave 1 spawns at 0:30. The first meeting is at about 0:52 in mid and 1:02 in side lanes (my calculation from the 22/32 s travel times).
  - Interval: 30 s until 14:00, 25 s from 14:00, 20 s from 30:00.
  - Cannon: from wave 3 (1:30), every 3rd wave, then every 2nd wave in the mid-game (15:05 per the older wikis, possibly 14:00 after 26.1), then every wave after 25:00.
  - Recall is 8 s. Empowered recall is about 4 s.
- Shorter intervals after 14:00 mean shorter free-time windows after a crash in the mid-game. Bounces come back sooner, so a mid-game 턴 is shorter, and recall and roam timing must be tighter. This is an inference; no source states it.
- Most English coaching content (cheater recall at "~3:00", cannon at "2:05") is out of date for 26.x. Everything moves about 35 s earlier.
