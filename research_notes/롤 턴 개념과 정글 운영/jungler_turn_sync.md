## 1. How coaches/guides describe jungle tempo, tracking and lane states ("which lanes can move first")

### Takeaway
Across 2025–2026 guides, "lane priority" means *which laner can leave lane first* because their wave is pushed or safe. That decides whether the jungler can scuttle, invade, gank or start an objective on that side. "Tempo" means converting time into an advantage before the next spawn timer. Tracking means predicting the enemy jungler so you play the opposite side.

### Cited Findings
- Lane priority is "the concept that determines which lane can move first". It ties to wave position, which decides whether a jungler can invade, contest Scuttle, start an objective, or take a step safely. Source: [YouTube, "What Is Lane Priority and Why It Wins Jungle Games"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zaIeft8oUts) (search summary).
- Boosteria's pathing guide has a "lane check": do the nearby lanes have priority to move first? Source: [Boosteria, LoL Jungle Pathing Basics: Tempo, Tracking & Gank Windows](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-pathing-basics-tempo-tracking-gank-windows).
- Lane states change fast, and "a plan made at 4 minutes can be stale by 8", so re-plan pathing as the game goes on. Source: [Level Legendary, Jungle Pathing and Tempo](https://levellegendary.com/jungle-pathing-tempo-gameplan/) (search summary paraphrase).
- Tempo, defined via objectives (snippet): "An early base before a Dragon timer converts gold into strength exactly when the map is about to price it". Unspent tempo "expires" at the next objective spawn. Source: [Disciplined Jungler, What Is Jungle Tempo?](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tempo/).
- Pathing that leaves you on the wrong side is a tempo error, not bad luck (snippet): "if your clear ends on the wrong side thirty seconds before dragon, you are not unlucky: your pathing put you out of tempo". Source: [Boosteria jungle guides](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-pathing-basics-tempo-tracking-gank-windows) (search attributes this to the Boosteria/Disciplined Jungler set; exact page not confirmed).
- MOBA Trainer (patch 26.13 page) uses lane priority to decide whether the jungler can take scuttle, invade, or ward deeper. It advises against forcing plays where the enemy has priority, and suggests bringing a laner along or checking the camp before an invade. Source: [MOBA Trainer, Ganking](https://www.mobatrainer.com/guides/ganking) (search summary; fetch blocked).
- Dignitas: once a jungler has priority, they can keep counter-jungling if they have the right vision. Lane priority converts into vision control and tempo. Source: [Dignitas, Transitioning Lane Priority into Tempo and Vision Control](https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/League-of-Legends/10280/transitioning-lane-priority-into-tempo-and-vision-control) (older article, undated in results).
- Eagz's free "TEMPO" Challenger course (video summary): junglers run their tempo on camps and objectives (dragon, Herald) rather than waves. Attacking the enemy's tempo (invading) requires being significantly stronger than the enemy jungler. Source: [videohighlight summary of Eagz TEMPO course](https://videohighlight.com/v/BL3UGSMM-P0).
- Tracking means predicting the enemy's next move so you make safer decisions. A jungler who shows on one side frees up the opposite side. Sources: [games.gg jungle guide](https://games.gg/league-of-legends/guides/league-of-legends-jungle-guide/); [Mobalytics jungler tips](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/tips-for-climbing-ladder-jungler-league-of-legends/) (search summary).
- Boosting Market's tracking guide estimates that after the enemy jungler ganks bot, "the opposite side gets roughly 20 to 30 seconds of tempo" for a play or an objective. Source: [Boosting Market, LoL Jungle Tracking](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-jungle-tracking-guide/).
- Korean community (namu/op.gg/dcinside summaries):
  - At dragon timing, check whether bot has priority, then decide between dragon and camps (snippet): "용 타이밍에는 바텀 라인의 주도권이 있는지 확인하고, 용을 칠지 캠프를 돌지 결정합니다. 바텀 라인이 불리할 경우, 용을 치기보다는 다른 라인에서 이득을 보는 것이 좋습니다."
  - If no lane has priority, put your weight behind one lane to make an objective possible.
  - Against a frozen wave, the snippet says not to force a gank but to "웨이브를 타워까지 밀어 넣을때까지 라인 근처에서 백업".
  - Sources: [나무위키 정글(리그 오브 레전드)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%A0%95%EA%B8%80(%EB%A6%AC%EA%B7%B8%20%EC%98%A4%EB%B8%8C%20%EB%A0%88%EC%A0%84%EB%93%9C)); [DCinside "정글학개론" (2019–2020 era, older)](https://gall.dcinside.com/board/view/?id=leagueoflegends3&no=7330222); [OP.GG Talk tip](https://talk.op.gg/s/lol/tip/877809/). The search tool did not say which quote came from which page.

### Inferences
- Operational definition for a "turn": a window during which a set of lanes have pushed waves (priority) so they can move. The jungler's camps/reset are timed so that he is full HP, has his items bought, and is on that side when the window opens.
- The core sequence the sources describe: lane state → which side has priority → enemy jungler's likely location → choose gank / farm / invade / objective.

## 2. Planning the 60–90 seconds before an objective spawns

### Takeaway
Guides converge on a countdown: reset (spend gold) ~90s out, clear camps *toward* the objective, have the nearby lanes shove/crash, ward the entrances ~30–60s out, and confirm priority. If priority isn't there, trade or skip rather than flip a coin.

### Cited Findings
- Boosteria (snippet): "One minute before it matters, do the setup checklist: reset, clear toward, vision, lane priority." Its other guide gives a 90/60/30-second plan: "Reset if you need to spend gold; you can't fight dragon with half-bought items." Sources: [Boosteria, Jungle Pathing Basics](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-pathing-basics-tempo-tracking-gank-windows); [Boosteria, Objective Priorities](https://boosteria.org/guides/comprehensive-guide-objective-management-in-league).
- Ward timing differs by source: refresh wards about 60s before spawn, or about 30s before, "ideally after pushing the nearby lane". Sources: [Boosteria jungle guide 2026](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-guide-for-beginners); [Disciplined Jungler](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tempo/). Which number came from which page is not confirmed.
- Don't spend both Smite charges on camps when Dragon spawns in 45 seconds. Source: same search set ([Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-guide-for-beginners) / [dodge.gg 2026](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/jungle-guide-2026)).
- Bot lane usually slow-pushes before Dragon, then crashes into the enemy tower. Crashing before recalling together keeps both players from losing the wave. "A crash you do nothing with is wasted." Sources: [mid.gg wave management](https://mid.gg/guides/wave-management); [Boostroom wave management 101 (2026)](https://boostroom.com/blog/wave-management-101-freeze-slow-push-crash-and-when-to-use-each).
- Caveat on the crash: track the enemy jungler before spending HP to force it. A greedy shove is a gank target. If you can't crash, take a defensive recall and accept the smaller loss. Source: [mid.gg recall timing](https://mid.gg/guides/recall-timing).
- Sometimes skipping is right: "giving up one dragon for Herald, plates or enemy camps is more valuable than dying in a river that was already lost." Source: [Boosteria objective guide](https://boosteria.org/guides/comprehensive-guide-objective-management-in-league) (search snippet).

### Inferences
- A practical template for a 5:00 dragon: full clear + scuttle → back around 3:45–4:00 → re-clear the bot-side camps → arrive at pit around 4:30 with bot/mid waves crashed. This is my synthesis from the scuttle/respawn timers in §4/§6, not a sourced timeline.

### Gaps

## 3. "Syncing backs" with laners

### Takeaway
Laners should ping their recall early and crash before backing. The jungler matches that timing so both come back together with items before the objective. The classic failure is a jungler who full-clears a side and recalls *as* the objective spawns.

### Cited Findings
- mid.gg: ping your recall timing early so the jungler can match it. Ideally recall at the same moment, after your wave has crashed. Back before every Dragon spawn rather than during it. Source: [mid.gg, When to Back: Recall Timing](https://mid.gg/guides/recall-timing).
- Anti-pattern: the jungler clears a full side and recalls as Dragon spawns, then arrives after the enemy has started it. Source: [mid.gg recall timing](https://mid.gg/guides/recall-timing) / [Kaizensclan bot lane recall timing, patch 26.15](https://kaizensclan.com/guides/guide-lol-bot-lane-recall-timing-26-15/) (search summary; which of the two said it is unconfirmed).
- Recall triggers (snippet): "backing on two triggers only – a spike your gold can actually buy, or a resource you've run out of – and only after you've crashed the wave into the enemy tower." Source: [Boosting Market, LoL Recall Timing](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-recall-timing-guide/).
- Junglers often die on the map holding 1200+ gold because they didn't reset. Source: search set incl. [Disciplined Jungler](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tempo/).
- Korean community: a laner who needs to back urgently may recall one wave early and leave the wave for the jungler to take (snippet): "귀환이 급한 라이너일 경우 한 타이밍 먼저 귀환을 하고 라인을 정글러에게 먹이기도 한다." Source: [나무위키 정글](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%A0%95%EA%B8%80(%EB%A6%AC%EA%B7%B8%20%EC%98%A4%EB%B8%8C%20%EB%A0%88%EC%A0%84%EB%93%9C)) / [DCinside 정글학개론](https://gall.dcinside.com/board/view/?id=leagueoflegends3&no=7330222).

### Inferences
- Sync rule of thumb: when bot lane crashes and backs about 60–90s before dragon, the jungler should back in the same window. He then paths through bot-side camps back to the pit, so all three arrive with fresh items.

### Gaps
- No quantified data (e.g., win rates when jungle and bot backs are synced) was found.

## 4. Scuttle crab and first clear tempo

### Takeaway
Scuttle spawns at 3:30 in current guides (older guides say 3:15). Contest it when the nearest lanes (mid plus bot or top on that side) have priority or you have a level lead. Otherwise path to the other scuttle or gank. A typical full clear finishes around 3:15–3:30 at level 4.

### Cited Findings
- First Scuttle spawns at 3:30 and respawns about 2:30 after being killed. Older Sportskeeda S11–S13 guides list 3:15. Sources: [League Coach Merch, Best Jungle Clear Path 2026](https://www.leaguecoachmerch.com/best-jungle-clear-path-2026/); [Wombo Combo, Jungle Pathing 101](https://www.wombocombo.gg/blog/game-fundamentals/jungle-pathing-101); outdated: [Sportskeeda S13](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/guide-jungle-pathing-league-legends-season-13); [Quizlet card (3:15, undated)](https://quizlet.com/565994607/jungle-camp-spawnrespawn-times-flash-cards/).
- Example blue-side route: Blue, Gromp, Wolves, Raptors, Red, Krugs, then contest Scuttle. A clean clear finishes around 3:15–3:30. Source: [Wombo Combo / Buyboosting pathing guides](https://buyboosting.com/blog/jungle-pathing-and-clear-routes-league-of-legends/) (search summary).
- Contest rule (snippet): contest "when you have lane priority from the nearest lane — if your mid laner can rotate faster than the enemy mid laner, you win the 2v1". Winning the Scuttle fight "often requires lane priority from your mid laner or bot lane". Level rule: if you're level 4 and the enemy is level 3 when Scuttle spawns, you have a significant fighting advantage and should contest aggressively. Sources: [dodge.gg Jungle Guide 2026](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/jungle-guide-2026); [Boosting Market jungle camps](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-jungle-camps-gold-value/) (search summary).
- First gank window: around 3:00–3:30 after three camps; a level-3 gank around 2:30–3:00. Full clears suit scaling junglers, faster level-3 ganks suit early-game junglers. Sources: [Buyboosting pathing](https://buyboosting.com/blog/jungle-pathing-and-clear-routes-league-of-legends/); [lolsuggest pathing](https://lolsuggest.com/blog/jungle-pathing-guide.html).
- First back: after three camps into gank/scuttle, or after a full clear plus scuttle. Back once you can afford an early item plus potions and a control ward. Jungle's early spike is about 500–1,000 gold (pet upgrade, component, control ward). A full clear plus scuttle puts you at level 4 around 3:30–4:00. Sources: [Buyboosting](https://buyboosting.com/blog/jungle-pathing-and-clear-routes-league-of-legends/); [Boosting Market recall timing](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-recall-timing-guide/).
- Korean community, if not full-clearing before the first back (snippet): "'이득보기 > 아군 정글몹 사냥 > 귀환' 이 순으로 짜는 것이 좋음". Source: [DCinside 정글학개론 (2019–2020, older)](https://gall.dcinside.com/board/view/?id=leagueoflegends3&no=7330222).

### Inferences
- Decision tree at about 3:15: is the scuttle-side mid/bot (or top) pushed, and am I equal or ahead in level? If yes, contest. If not, take the opposite scuttle, gank the lane where the enemy is overextended, or back.

### Gaps
- Exact current scuttle respawn (2:30) and spawn (3:30) not verified on the LoL Wiki (blocked).

## 5. Cross-mapping and objective trades

### Takeaway
If the enemy jungler shows on one side, take the objective or camps on the opposite side. Don't chase and don't flip a coin on a contested objective without priority. Trade grubs/herald for dragon (or the reverse), then contest the next one.

### Cited Findings
- Definition (snippet): cross-map "consists in making a reactive play on the opposite side of the map to where the enemy jungler is. If he's the top side, you make a bot lane play and vice-versa." It is a higher-level concept. Source: [esports.net, How to jungle in LoL](https://www.esports.net/news/lol/how-to-jungle-lol/).
- Rule (snippet): "if the enemy jungler shows top, take dragon. If they show bot, take Herald or grubs." Variant: "Enemy ganks bot → you take top-side objective or invade top camps, enemy shows top → you take dragon + bot camps." Sources: [LoL Theory blog](https://blog.loltheory.gg/how-to-play-jungle-lol/); [Boosteria jungle guide 2026](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-guide-for-beginners).
- "Trade sides, never chase." Source: [Level Legendary, Vertical Jungling](https://levellegendary.com/vertical-jungling-splitting-the-map/).
- Grubs window (snippet): "The 8:00–14:45 window is when you should be looking for opportunities to take them, ideally when you have top or mid priority." Source: [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-guide-for-beginners) (search summary).
- Trade suggestion: take Grubs while the enemy takes Dragon, then contest the next Dragon. Source: search set ([Boosteria objective guide](https://boosteria.org/guides/comprehensive-guide-objective-management-in-league)).
- "Starting dragon while bot/mid cannot move leads to deaths and lost tempo." Source: [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-guide-for-beginners).

### Inferences
- Trade value depends on the patch: grubs no longer respawn in a second wave (see §6), and Atakhan is removed in 2026. The value of a top-side trade is therefore concentrated in one 8:00 grub set and one 15:00 Herald.

## 6. Current-season objective spawn times and camp respawns (Season 2026 / patch 26.x)

### Takeaway
Season 2026 (patch 26.1, Jan 8 2026) **removed Atakhan** (plus Blood Roses and Feats of Strength) and moved **Baron back to 20:00**. Other timers per 2026 guides:
- **Void Grubs:** 8:00, one group of three, no respawn (since 25.09).
- **Dragon:** 5:00, then 5:00 after each kill.
- **Rift Herald:** 15:00, despawns 19:45.
- **Baron:** 20:00, 6:00 respawn.
- **Elder:** after soul, 6:00 respawn.
- **Scuttle:** 3:30, 2:30 respawn.
- **Small camps:** 2:15. **Buffs:** 5:00.

### Cited Findings
- Riot patch 26.1: Atakhan "has been removed from the game and will no longer spawn". Blood Roses and Feats of Strength were also removed, to reduce an "extremely objective-focused game". Baron gets an Elder-like treatment (reduced overall gold reward), and its spawn no longer corrupts Red/Blue buff. Sources: [Riot, Patch 26.1 Notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/); [esports.gg, Atakhan/Feats removed in 2026](https://esports.gg/news/league-of-legends/atakhan-feats-of-strength-league-of-legends-2026/); [Sportskeeda 26.1 notes](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/league-legends-patch-26-1-notes).
- Baron: "25:00 ⇒ 20:00" ([Sportskeeda 26.1](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/league-legends-patch-26-1-notes)). **Conflict:** the search snippet of Riot's official page read "Baron Nashor now spawns at 12:00", which is probably a snippet/typo error. Every other source (Sportskeeda, nerfplz S16, games.gg) says 20:00. Verify on the [Riot page](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/).
- Void Grubs: spawn at 8:00 as one group of three and never respawn (since patch 25.09). Sources: [gamerstogether.cz LoL Jungle 2026](https://gamerstogether.cz/en/lol-jungle-objectives-guide/); [games.gg Season 2026 guide](https://games.gg/league-of-legends/guides/league-of-legends-season-2026-everything-new-demacia/). - Rift Herald: 15:00, takes the Baron pit until 19:45; Baron replaces it at 20:00 and respawns 6 minutes after each kill. Dragon: first at 5:00, then 5:00 after each drake. ## 7. Common jungler tempo mistakes (cross-cutting)

### Cited Findings
- Ganking blind ("The worst habit is ganking without knowing where the enemy jungler is"). Sources: search set [dodge.gg 2026](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/jungle-guide-2026) / [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-guide-for-beginners).

Starting dragon without bot/mid priority. Sources: [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-jungle-pathing-basics-tempo-tracking-gank-windows), [mid.gg](https://mid.gg/guides/recall-timing), [Disciplined Jungler](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tempo/).
- Forcing ganks into frozen waves instead of helping the laner shove (Korean community). Source: [나무위키 정글](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%A0%95%EA%B8%80(%EB%A6%AC%EA%B7%B8%20%EC%98%A4%EB%B8%8C%20%EB%A0%88%EC%A0%84%EB%93%9C)).
