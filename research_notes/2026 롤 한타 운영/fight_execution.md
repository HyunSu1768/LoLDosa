## 1. Target selection: "hit what you can reach" vs diving the carry, threat assessment, focusing, peel

### Takeaway
The 2025-2026 coaching consensus is "hit the closest target you can hit safely." Only divers and assassins should go for the backline, and only after the enemy has used its CC and peel. Peel is the same CC used defensively, after an enemy diver commits.

### Cited Findings
- Boostroom (2026, "LoL Teamfighting Basics 2026: Targets & Spacing") rule: hit the closest safe target; switch only if the new target is as safe or safer; when unsure, fall back to that rule. — [Boostroom](https://boostroom.com/blog/teamfighting-basics-target-selection-spacing-and-win-conditions)
- Boostroom role layout for a front-to-back fight: the frontline holds space, mid/control champions cover the entrances, the marksman stays at max safe range hitting the closest safe target, and the assassin waits for a backline angle until enemy CC is committed. — [Boostroom](https://boostroom.com/blog/teamfighting-basics-target-selection-spacing-and-win-conditions)
- Metabot: deciding who to focus "isn't about killing the scariest champion first — it's about killing whatever you can hit without leaving your spot." Positioning means the spot where you deal the most damage while taking the least. — [Metabot](https://metabot.gg/en/league/guides/teamfight-positioning-guide)
- MOBA Trainer (patch 26.10/26.13 hub) lists the predictable errors: a botlaner walks past their own frontline, an assassin dives into peel instead of waiting, a tank engages without follow-up. It calls poor positioning "the number one reason carries get one-shot." — [MOBA Trainer](https://www.mobatrainer.com/guides/teamfighting); [MOBA Trainer learn page](https://mobatrainer.com/learn/teamfighting)
- Mobalytics (updated Oct 2025): tanks should always be toward the front of their team; assassins (for example LeBlanc) usually flank to reach a squishy target. — [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-die-less-in-teamfights/)
- LoL Theory: peel is a subset of disengage that breaks the enemy's engage. The same CC is an engage or a peel depending on timing and target: a stun before the fight is engage, the same stun after a diver reaches your carry is peel. — [LoL Theory – Peeling](https://blog.loltheory.gg/what-is-peeling-league-of-legends/); [LoL Theory – Engage & Disengage](https://blog.loltheory.gg/engage-disengage-lol/)
- Dodge.gg Tank Guide (Mar 23, 2026) splits tanks into "Vanguards" (look for engages) and "Wardens" (look to peel). Some champions can do both. — [Dodge.gg](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/tank-guide-2026)
- Front-to-back plans rely on hitting the enemy frontline first once the fight starts. — [The Backdash](https://www.thebackdash.com/league-of-legends/what-is-a-front-to-back-playstyle-in-lol)
- Korean community advice (Inven guide, older): rank the enemy targets 1st to 5th ("누구부터 물지 1위부터 5위까지") before the fight. — search summary of [Inven manual](https://lol.inven.co.kr/dataninfo/champion/manualToolView.php?idx=133703) and [redwendy](https://redwendy.com/archives/678) (older community posts, flagged)

### Inferences
- The WHY behind "closest safe target": damage only counts while you are alive and in range. Walking past the frontline to reach the carry puts you in range of the whole enemy team's CC, so your total damage drops to zero. That is why every source ties target choice to position.
- For a Platinum jungler the rule depends on the champion's class. Tank or engage junglers (Vanguard): start the fight or peel. Bruiser junglers: hit the frontline or flank targets you can reach. Assassin junglers (Kha'Zix, Rengar, Nocturne...): wait for key CC and peel to be used, then go for the backline. Diving "the carry" first is correct only for that last group, and only after the trigger.
- Threat assessment rule for the app: if the closest reachable enemy is a fed carry or out of position, focus them; if it is a tank and your team is front-to-back, keep hitting it while carries do damage.

## 2. Engage triggers and disengage triggers

### Takeaway
Engage on an advantage: more numbers, key enemy cooldowns used (Flash, ults, mobility, defensive spells), vision, and favorable wave and HP state. Do not engage just because your own ult is up. Disengage when the enemy lands its key ult on your carries or when the advantage disappears.

### Cited Findings
- A numbers advantage is the most basic rule of a fight. The same logic applies to 4v3, 3v2 and 2v1. — search summary of [Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/guides/how-to-team-fight-in-league-of-legends) / [racoon.gg](https://racoon.gg/league-of-legends/blog/league-of-legends-teamfighting-guide) / [League Coach Merch](https://www.leaguecoachmerch.com/improve-team-fighting/) (summary combined these sources; Hotspawn piece is older, flagged)
- Second layer, cooldowns: check whether the enemy used ultimates, mobility, defensive abilities or Summoner Spells. A burnt Flash (300 s cooldown) is "a massive window of opportunity." — same source set as above; [Mobalytics – How to Engage](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/how-to-engage-league-of-legends/) (older, about 2020, flagged)
- Pressing your ult the moment it comes up is the most common solo-queue engage mistake. Engage on an advantage, never just because the ult is up. — search summary of the 2026 guides above ([Dodge.gg](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/tank-guide-2026) / [Boosting Market](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-teamfighting-guide/))
- Dodge.gg tank guide (2026): if the enemy ADC flashed 30 seconds ago, they are a free target for your engage. A well-timed engage decides games and a badly timed one loses them. — [Dodge.gg](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/tank-guide-2026)
- Pre-commit checklist: are teammates nearby, do you have vision, are your key abilities up, has the enemy used its key cooldowns. — search summary of [Boosting Market](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-teamfighting-guide/) / [Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/guide/how-to-teamfight-in-league-of-legends)
- Disengage trigger: if the enemy lands a massive ultimate on your carries, disengage. — same source set
- LoL Theory "soft engage": even if the tank's engage misses its intended target, the team commits anyway and the carry is freed to peel or reset. — [LoL Theory](https://blog.loltheory.gg/engage-disengage-lol/)
- MOBA Trainer: scan the scoreboard and minimap every 30 seconds for fight windows (summoners down, deaths, item completions). Advanced content covers terrain, vision and flank TP. — [MOBA Trainer](https://www.mobatrainer.com/guides/teamfighting)
- Wave state before objectives: dragon and herald fights are decided in lane minutes before the spawn. "If bot cannot move first, you are coinflipping a 4v5." Set up waves about 1:20 before the objective timer. — [Upforge](https://upforge.gg/blog/lol-mistakes-ranked-players-fix) or [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-teamfight-fundamentals-front-back-flanks-focus) (the summary did not say which of the two; blog-level)
- Leaving lane on a crashing wave is the most common low-elo macro mistake. A shoving wave lets you rotate without losing ground. — [Boosting Market – Macro](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-macro-guide/)
- Before pushing, check the enemy jungler's last known position or ward the river: "Fast pushing blind... is how laners die right before an objective." — [mid.gg](https://mid.gg/guides/wave-management)
- Vision timing varies by source: about 30 s before spawn (warding at spawn is "usually too late"), about two waves before spawn, or 60-90 s before Baron. The jungler clears wards and invades to remove vision, which lets supports ward safely. — [Victory View](https://victoryview.gg/blog/vision-score-in-lol-what-it-means-for-your-team); [Boosting Market – Vision](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-vision-score-guide/); [Messy Modding warding](https://www.messymoddingstore.com/blog/league-of-legends-warding-guide) (the sources conflict on exact windows)
- Korean community: 후진입 (late entry) after dodging the enemy's one hard CC can work even 1v3; 선진입 (going in first) only works in small fights or with specific items. — search summary of [Inven manual](https://m.inven.co.kr/site/lol/manualtool.php?idx=133703) and [FMKorea Irelia post](https://www.fmkorea.com/3033475095) (champion-specific community posts, older, flagged)
- Korean community: if your level or items are behind, delay the fight even with a good composition. — search summary of [redwendy](https://redwendy.com/archives/678) (older blog, flagged)

### Inferences
- An engage-checklist order the app can show the jungler: (1) numbers (count enemies on the map / missing), (2) enemy Flash and key ults down (300 s Flash window), (3) your team present (no staggered arrival) and your own cooldowns up, (4) vision of the fight area, (5) waves (bot/mid pushed so they can move first), (6) HP/mana.
- Disengage triggers: an enemy key ult lands on your carry; a teammate dies first with no trade; an enemy you lost track of shows up (the 5th member); your engage misses and you have no "soft engage" follow-up.

### Gaps
- No primary-source statistic for how much winning a fight improves with a numbers advantage, or with an HP/mana lead.

## 3. Fight types (front-to-back, dive/flank, pick, poke/siege, skirmish) and matchups

### Takeaway
Front-to-back protects carries and grinds through the frontline but loses to early dive. Dive skips the frontline to remove the main damage source. Pick catches a lone target before the fight. Poke/siege lowers HP before forcing objectives and beats disengage, but loses to hard engage. Disengage beats engage. These are heuristic cycles, not fixed rules.

### Cited Findings
- Front-to-back: a pure teamfighting comp that protects carries while the rest of the team peels. It struggles against dive comps that snowball off early leads. — [Mobalytics team comps](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/guides/everything-you-need-to-know-about-team-comps-and-teamfighting); [The Backdash](https://www.thebackdash.com/league-of-legends/complete-guide-to-team-compositions-in-lol)
- Dive: collapse past the frontline and erase the enemy's main damage source. "Leave no place safe for your team." — [The Backdash](https://www.thebackdash.com/league-of-legends/complete-guide-to-team-compositions-in-lol)
- Engage/hard engage starts decisive fights and collapses before the opponent resets. It counters poke ("they don't have many tools if you engage"). — [Mobalytics team comps](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/guides/everything-you-need-to-know-about-team-comps-and-teamfighting)
- Poke/siege: poke down health bars first, then force objectives; siege hits turrets while the enemy defends. Poke beats disengage because "if you do not have anything to engage on, it negates the disengage." — [Mobalytics team comps](https://mobalytics.gg/lol/guides/everything-you-need-to-know-about-team-comps-and-teamfighting) / [Dignitas](https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Unknown/10938/the-five-team-compositions-of-league-of-legends)
- Disengage is strong against engage because it can defer fights. Poke/siege and split-push comps need expert teamwork and communication. — [Dignitas – Five Team Compositions](https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Unknown/10938/the-five-team-compositions-of-league-of-legends) (older, flagged)
- Counter cycle (Nerfplz, 2016, flagged as old but still cited): Poke < Sustain < All-In < Disengage < Poke. — [Nerfplz](https://www.nerfplz.com/2016/12/team-composition-building-101.html)
- Another scheme: Engage beats Disengage and Pick, and loses to Split Push and Poke/Siege. — [lol-tracker](https://lol-tracker.com/compositions) / [Guild Order](https://guildorder.com/games/league/guides/draft-and-champ-select-theory) (the two schemes conflict on Engage vs Disengage, see note)
- Flank: Boosteria's 2026-era fundamentals page covers "Front-to-Back, Flanks & Focus." Snippet only. — [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-teamfight-fundamentals-front-back-flanks-focus)

### Inferences
- Conflict note: Dignitas and Nerfplz say disengage beats engage, but one draft guide lists engage as beating disengage. In solo queue, treat disengage as beating engage only when the engaging team commits into it without numbers or cooldown advantage.
- Matchup playbook (inferred from the cited archetype definitions):
  - Front-to-back vs front-to-back: whichever team has stronger carries or items fights in open space, holds formation and hits the closest target. The jungler's job is to engage or peel based on champion class.
  - Front-to-back vs dive: the front-to-back team keeps the frontline close to the carry and saves peel CC for the diver. The dive team waits for that CC and goes in from the side or flank.
  - Your team poke vs their engage: hold vision, keep spacing, poke before the objective spawns and do not let them get into range. Their team: force a fast engage through a flank or fog of war.
  - Pick: play around vision and fog of war; catch a lone enemy before the objective and then start the objective 5v4.
  - Skirmish (2v2/3v3): numbers, levels and cooldowns decide it. The jungler's arrival is the "numbers" lever.

### Gaps

## 4. Common Platinum teamfight mistakes and fixes

### Takeaway
The repeated low-elo errors are: fighting without vision or numbers (4v5), staggered arrival, engaging just because an ult is up, carries walking past their frontline, assassins diving into peel, tanks engaging without follow-up, chasing, and leaving lane on a crashing wave before an objective.

### Cited Findings
- Botlaner walks past frontline / assassin dives into peel / tank engages without follow-up. — [MOBA Trainer](https://mobatrainer.com/learn/teamfighting)
- Ult-on-cooldown engage as the top solo-queue engage mistake. — [Dodge.gg](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/tank-guide-2026) / [Boosting Market](https://boostingmarket.com/blogs/lol-teamfighting-guide/) (summary)
- "If bot cannot move first, you are coinflipping a 4v5." — [Upforge](https://upforge.gg/blog/lol-mistakes-ranked-players-fix) / [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/lol-teamfight-fundamentals-front-back-flanks-focus)
- At 4v5, "You can't fight at 4v5, but you can prevent the enemy from setting up the next objective uncontested" (i.e., trade or ward instead). — [Victory View](https://victoryview.gg/blog/vision-score-in-lol-what-it-means-for-your-team)
- Staggered commitment as the most common way fights are lost, and chasing "the enemy who is easiest to see rather than easiest to kill." — [rank-climb-ai (generic, possibly Overwatch-oriented; low reliability)](https://rank-climb-ai.lovable.app/guides/how-to-make-better-decisions-during-team-fights)
- Korean community: even if the 딜각 looks perfect, don't get excited; deal damage from a safe distance using range. — search summary of [Inven Twitch manual](https://lol.inven.co.kr/dataninfo/champion/manualToolView.php?idx=133703) (older, flagged)

### Inferences
- Fix mapping for the app: no vision → ward or sweep 60-90 s before (jungler sweeps); staggered → group then go, or don't start; chasing → stop chasing when the target leaves your team's range or reaches fog of war or towers, and turn to the objective; 4v5 → don't contest, trade a cross-map objective; wrong target → closest safe target.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 Platinum-specific statistical study of teamfight mistakes found. A strong source for "chasing" in LoL specifically was not found (only a generic or low-reliability one).

## 5. Fight timing: item spikes, level 6/11/16, recognizing "we're stronger now"

### Takeaway
Power spikes are relative to the opponent and are really "recall spikes." Early-game comps peak before second items; scaling comps peak when builds complete. Level 6 is the biggest early spike; level 16 (rank 3 ult) matters for late-game teamfight champions.

### Cited Findings
- Power spike = a jump in your strength against a specific opponent. Track one level and one item spike rather than a long list. An item spike is really a recall spike; a spike used at minute 9 is worth more than the same spike at minute 12. Early-game comps peak before the enemy finishes second items; scaling comps peak once builds complete. Level 16 = final ult rank, mostly relevant to late-game teamfight champions. — [Nerfplz – Power Spikes Explained (Sep 2026)](https://www.nerfplz.com/2026/09/power-spikes-explained-when-you-are.html)
- Hitting level 6 before the enemy laner gives a large advantage. — search summary of [Mobalytics – How to Engage](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/how-to-engage-league-of-legends/) (older, flagged)
- Completed components give stats before the full item; recall with a component when slightly short. The current patch raised Voltaic Cyclosword to 3000g. — [Buildzcrank 2026 itemization](https://buildzcrank.com/en/blog/lol-itemization-guide-2026/)
- Crit ADCs: Infinity Edge is strongest as a second or third item, not first. — [Dodge.gg ADC items 2026](https://www.dodge.gg/en-US/lol/news/attack-speed-crit-items-2026)
- 2026 season item changes summary. — [Dignitas – new items 2026](https://dignitas.gg/articles/a-guide-on-all-new-items-in-season-2026-of-league-of-legends)

### Inferences
- "We are stronger right now" signals the app can compute: level lead around 6/11/16 thresholds, completed-item count and gold lead per lane, enemy Flash and key ult cooldowns, numbers on the map. Because spikes decay, the window to force fights is right after the spike (after a recall), before the enemy answers with its own.

## 6. Statistics on fight and game outcomes

### Takeaway
I found no study that directly measures teamfight win rate based on first death or numbers advantage. The adjacent studies treat first blood as a modest, early-stage signal.

### Cited Findings
- Real-time prediction paper (2023): first blood and kills matter mainly in early stages. — [arXiv 2309.02449](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.02449)
- CMU capstone (2024, Diamond–Master games): first blood is a minor factor; kill and death totals are more informative. — [CMU](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/capstoneresearch/spring2024/460files/team17.pdf)
- About 5,000 major-league games since 2020: blue-side bot concedes first blood in 19.6% of games vs 17.7% red. — [Itero](https://www.itero.gg/articles/side-selection)
- Riot/AWS live win-probability stat in broadcasts since MSI/Worlds 2023 (methodology not detailed). — [AWS blog](https://aws.amazon.com/cn/blogs/china/riot-games-and-aws-bring-esports-win-probability-stat-to-2023-league-of-legends-world-championships-broadcasts)

### Inferences
- The app can compute its own numbers from its DuckDB timeline data: for fights clustered from kill events, P(win fight | first death), P(win | numbers advantage), and P(win | gold lead). That would be stronger evidence than anything found online.

### Gaps
- No per-fight statistic on first death, numbers advantage or gold lead was found; Oracle's Elixir or the app's own timeline data would be needed.

- 딜각 used for a window to deal damage safely. — [Inven Twitch manual](https://lol.inven.co.kr/dataninfo/champion/manualToolView.php?idx=133703)

### Inferences (unverified common usage; must verify before shipping)
- 앞뒤 한타 (앞뒤 싸움): front-to-back fight, where both teams' frontlines meet and carries deal damage from behind. NOTE: one search summary defined it as "attacking from front and back at the same time (협공)". That reading likely conflicts with mainstream usage; verify on Namuwiki.
- 진입: going into the enemy formation (선진입 = first in, 후진입 = in after enemy CC is used).
- 이니시(에이팅): starting the fight with engage tools; 이니시 챔프 = engager.
- 받아치기: counter-engage, fighting back into the enemy's engage, usually after their key skills miss or are used.
- 딜각: a window where a carry can deal damage safely.
- 포커싱: focusing one target with the team's damage.
- 필링: peel (protecting carries from divers). The search summarizer could not confirm it, but it is the transliteration of "peel(ing)".
- 짤라먹기 (짜르기): picking off a separated enemy before their team arrives (pick).
- 물렸다: "got caught" by enemy CC or engage, unable to escape.
- 각: an opportunity, window or angle (킬각, 한타각, 이니시각).
