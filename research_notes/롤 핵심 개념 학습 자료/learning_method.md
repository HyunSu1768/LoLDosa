## 1. What should Platinum junglers prioritize first? (skill priority order)

### Takeaway
No source I could reach gives a coach-authored priority list specific to Platinum. The consensus across coaching and community sources puts things in this order: (1) pathing and clear efficiency plus a gank/farm balance, (2) tracking the enemy jungler as a running prediction, (3) objective setup tied to lane priority, (4) a narrow champion pool. Mechanics-heavy champions are discouraged.

### Cited Findings
- [OPINION] Mobalytics says low-ELO junglers "tend to avoid ganking early and barely ever have a balance between ganking and farming." — [Mobalytics (search snippet)](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-how-to-climb-low-elo-jungler/)
- [OPINION] Mobalytics: "the biggest problem low ELO players have is that they try to take on overly complex champions such as Lee Sin and Nidalee". It also says "jungle pathing can be quite tricky for new junglers to understand." Note that Mobalytics defines "low elo" inconsistently: one jungle article means roughly Gold and below, while a Wild Rift guide means below Emerald. — [Mobalytics low elo jungler](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-how-to-climb-low-elo-jungler/); [Mobalytics Low ELO to Diamond jungler pt.1](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/low-elo-to-diamond-jungler-part-1/)
- [OPINION] Low-ELO players don't buy Control Wards, which makes junglers like Evelynn hard to spot. — [Mobalytics (search snippet)](https://mobalytics.gg/?p=22141)
- [OPINION] r/summonerschool lists two common jungle mistakes: "pathing towards camps instead of opportunities" and "forcing objectives without lane priority." — [r/summonerschool mirror](https://r.datuan.dev/r/summonerschool)
- [OPINION] WeCoach treats anticipation, quick decisions and efficient pathing as core principles. It adds that each camp gives different XP and gold, so a coach helps balance fast levels against steady gold. — [WeCoach jungle coaching](https://wecoach.gg/blog/article/everything-you-need-to-know-about-league-of-legends-jungle-coaching)
- [OPINION] The 1v9 Academy jungle course is structured as "Proper Jungling Blueprint • Objectives 101 • Ganking 101 • Invades 101." — [1v9 Academy (Skool)](https://www.skool.com/1v9)
- [OPINION, KR] An FMKorea thread says beginners should learn basic route planning before trying to track the enemy jungler. This supports putting pathing ahead of tracking. — [FMKorea](https://www.fmkorea.com/6240920572)
- [OPINION, KR] An Inven/OP.GG Platinum-escape tip summarized in search results: "귀환 후 탬을 사고 찰나의 순간동안 이번 턴은 어떤 동선을 밟고 상대 정글의 움직임을 조심스럽게 예상해본다." (After backing and buying, take a moment to plan this turn's route and predict the enemy jungler's movement.) — [Inven: 플레티넘 탈출하는 팁](http://www.inven.co.kr/board/lol/2766/33930); [OP.GG: 정글로 플레 탈출 비법](https://talk.op.gg/s/lol/user-news/221892/%EC%A0%95%EA%B8%80%EB%A1%9C%20%ED%94%8C%EB%A0%88%20%ED%83%88%EC%B6%9C%20%EB%B9%84%EB%B2%95); [OP.GG: 플레티넘 탈출하는 법](https://talk.op.gg/s/lol/tip/1996415/%ED%94%8C%EB%A0%88%ED%8B%B0%EB%84%98-%ED%83%88%EC%B6%9C%ED%95%98%EB%8A%94-%EB%B2%95-%EC%95%8C%EB%A0%A4%EB%93%9C%EB%A6%BD%EB%8B%88%EB%8B%A4)
- [OPINION, KR] "정글과의 커뮤니케이션을 확실히 하고 자주하라" (Communicate with your jungler clearly and often), from a Korean Platinum-escape tip post. Many of these Korean posts are from 2014–2017, so they may be dated. — [Inven 팁 게시판](http://www.inven.co.kr/board/lol/2766/33930)
- [OPINION, Riot] Riot's 2023 preseason jungle dev post says jungling should leave room for skill expression. — [Riot: 2023 프리시즌 정글 현황](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/ko-kr/news/dev/preseason-2023-state-of-the-jungle/)

### Inferences
- A defensible priority order for Platinum: ① A planned 0–3:30 path, with a full clear or a gank chosen based on lane state. ② Tracking: predict the enemy jungler from their start, clear speed and CS. ③ Objective timing with lane priority (don't force it without priority). ④ Narrow the champion pool to simple, consistent junglers. ⑤ Turn planning on every back. This is a synthesis across sources, not a single coach's list.

- I found no data source that ranks which skills move Platinum junglers' win rate most. Mobalytics GPI weightings were not accessible.

## 2. Replay review (복기) methods and checklists

### Takeaway
Coaching sources agree on a few rules: review with intent, pick 1–2 themes per review instead of listing every error, and focus on decision points. Jungle reviews should check the 0–4 minute path, tracking inside the first 10 minutes, and objective setups. Korean community advice is to review your losses, especially the ones where the enemy jungler outplayed you.

### Cited Findings
- [OPINION] insights.gg advises against listing every error and recommends choosing two themes to fix. — [insights.gg: What Is VOD Review?](https://insights.gg/blog/what-is-vod-review)
- [OPINION] WeTeachLeague "How to Review 101" says reviews should be short ("less than five minutes" in the snippet) and that the value comes from deliberately reviewing mistakes and missed opportunities. — [WeTeachLeague](https://weteachleague.com/?p=531)
- [OPINION] WeCoach lays out a general process: set clear goals, select key moments, annotate, rewatch with intent, discuss alternatives, and track progress over time. It also notes that built-in replays expire with the patch, so you should record your own footage. — [WeCoach VOD reviews](https://wecoach.gg/blog/article/up-your-game-understanding-league-of-legends-vod-reviews)
- [OPINION] Jungle coaching sessions on Metafy focus on "how we have been applying basics and fundamentals and how are we reading the map," pathing, and "how are we investing ourselves in fights and skirmishes." — [Metafy nalkya](https://metafy.gg/@nalkya/sessions/coaching-session-W4vbc8KfpZH); [Metafy jungle menace](https://metafy.gg/@rubenxswrd/sessions/jungle-menace-yHycBNzZ0tr)
- [OPINION, KR] FMKorea recommends reviewing 3–5 lost games to see why the enemy jungler countered you. — [FMKorea 정글 잘하는 법](https://www.fmkorea.com/260370499)
- [OPINION] Tracking method for review: "three pieces of information place him on the map": the starting buff, the champion's clear speed, and the camp count shown by their CS. Update the estimate as new information appears. — [Disciplined Jungler: jungle tracking](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tracking/)
- [OPINION, KR] Namu Wiki says you can infer the enemy jungler's route from unseen areas and their CS when your team has no vision of them. — [나무위키 정글](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%A0%95%EA%B8%80(%EB%A6%AC%EA%B7%B8%20%EC%98%A4%EB%B8%8C%20%EB%A0%88%EC%A0%84%EB%93%9C))
- [OPINION, KR] An OP.GG long-form post on finding the enemy jungler early: laner HP and mana only hint at the route and can be faked, so place a ward in the enemy jungle early to confirm it. — [OP.GG 게임 초반 정글러 위치 파악하기](https://talk.op.gg/s/lol/tip/1570037/)
- [OPINION] Look for the "40-second absence": an enemy jungler who hasn't shown for about 40 seconds is worth actively tracking. Also, after the laning phase, jungle tracking becomes team tracking. — [Sulfurgame tracking guide](https://sulfurgame.com/enemy-jungle-tracking-guide/) (low-authority site)

### Inferences (synthesized checklist; not from a single source)
- **0:00–4:00 (path):** Was the starting side chosen with lane priority and the gank side in mind? Did the clear finish near 3:15–3:30? What did you do at the 3–4 minute decision point (gank, scuttle, or reset)? Where did you predict the enemy started?
- **0–10:00 (tracking):** At 2:00, 3:30, 5:00 and 7:00, pause and write where you think the enemy jungler is. Then reveal and compare. Use the enemy's start, clear speed and CS to make each guess.
- **Objectives (first dragon/grubs/herald):** Were lanes pushed before you started? Was vision placed 45–60 s before spawn? Was Smite tracked?
- **Each back:** Did you plan the next turn? Was the gold spent well?
- **Deaths:** Was it information you lacked, or information you had and ignored?
- Per the two-theme rule, pick only 1–2 categories per review.

### Gaps
- I found no study measuring how much replay review improves LoL performance.
- Skill Capped and ProGuides jungle VOD checklists could not be retrieved.

## 3. Practice and champion pool (1–3 champions?)

### Takeaway
The best quantitative evidence, an iTero/JackJ analysis of 1M+ games, shows win rate drops sharply when a player has little experience on a champion (about 44% under 10k mastery, roughly the first 20 games), then flattens at about 50–52%. Community consensus is 2–4 champions per role. Peer-reviewed esports research does **not** show that "deliberate practice" hours predict LoL performance. Structured "one focus per game" practice is coaching opinion, not evidence-backed.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA-BLOG] iTero mastery study of 1M+ solo queue games: mastery is "by far the most impactful" factor. Players under 10,000 mastery average about a 44% win rate, while "above 10k, the win rate hits 50% and above". At roughly 600 mastery per game, that is about 20 games before you stop dragging down your win chance on that champion. There's a plateau: players over 1M mastery had a 51.73% win rate versus 51.77% for 50K–1M. — [iTero: Mastery – a statistical summary of 1M+ games](https://www.itero.gg/articles/mastery-a-statistical-summary); [JackJ Substack mirror](https://jackjgaming.substack.com/p/mastery-a-statistical-summary-of)
- [DATA-BLOG] iTero's solo queue draft analysis of 1M+ games is related. — [iTero draft-sq](https://www.itero.gg/articles/draft-sq)
- [OPINION] iTero: "if you want to climb, limit your Champion Pool." It also acknowledges some factors that favor a larger pool. — [iTero champion pool builder](https://itero.gg/articles/champion-pool-builder)
- [DATA, secondary] RiftFeed and Mein-MMO report that Riot examined whether one-tricks inflate champion win rates and found the effect small. A cited Reddit post says only Katarina rose, by 0.4%. I did not verify this against Riot's original statement. — [RiftFeed](https://riftfeed.gg/lol-news/lol-myth-debunked-by-riot-after-13-years-riftfeed); [Mein-MMO](https://mein-mmo.de/lol-main-charaktere-schwierig-staerker/)
- [OPINION, KR] "자신이 주로 가는 라인에 챔피언은 2-3개에서 많게는 3-4개 정도가 적당하다" (2–3, at most 3–4, champions for your main role is about right). Another Korean post says jungle picks work better when split by role, because playing only similar champions is inefficient. — [OP.GG 심해탈출 #1](https://talk.op.gg/s/lol/user-news/209224/); [Inven 좋은 정글 챔프폭 구성](https://www.inven.co.kr/board/lol/3371/573606)
- [OPINION, KR] A Korean YouTube jungle-climb video lists "챔프폭 줄여라" (narrow your champion pool) as its first tip. — [YouTube 롤 정글 잘하는 5가지 방법](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EomkUaGrXjk)
- [EVIDENCE] Martončik et al. (2024, *Collabra: Psychology*, registered report on psychological predictors of long-term esports success) found **no** meaningful effect of deliberate practice on LoL performance: r ≈ −.01 against a hypothesized r > .2. Non-deliberate practice hours predicted performance (r ≈ .26), and younger age predicted better performance (r ≈ −.22). Figures come from the search summary; I could not fetch the full text. — [PCI RR recommendation](https://rr.peercommunityin.org/articles/rec?id=269); [JYX repository](https://jyx.jyu.fi/handle/123456789/95712?show=full); [Ewha Pure record](https://pure.ewha.ac.kr/en/publications/psychological-predictors-of-long-term-esports-success-a-registere/)
- [EVIDENCE] Valls-Serrano et al. (2022, *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*): attention and visuospatial working memory separated expert from regular players. The authors caution that time-based definitions of expertise are imprecise. — [Frontiers 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2022.933331/epub); [PMC9351611](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351611)
- [EVIDENCE] A large-sample LoL study (n = 162,417) looked at how practice distribution (spacing) relates to performance over time. I could not retrieve its details. — [PMC9565695](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9565695/)
- [EVIDENCE, qualitative] Abbott et al. (2022, *Journal of Electronic Gaming and Esports*) used the deliberate practice framework to study how LoL players and teams perceive effective training. — [Abbott et al. 2022 PDF](https://eprints.chi.ac.uk/6532/1/Abbott%20et%20al.%20%282022%29%20-%20Perceptions%20of%20effective%20training.pdf)

### Inferences
- The mastery data supports focusing on 1–3 champions: the first ~20 games on a champion cost win rate, and depth past that adds little. Spreading games across many champions means spending more games in the low-mastery zone.
- "One focus per game" fits general skill-acquisition advice, but the only LoL-specific peer-reviewed test found that "deliberate practice" as measured did not predict rank. Present focused practice as coaching consensus, not a proven method.

### Gaps
- I found no study that directly measures champion pool *size* against win rate. Only mastery per champion is measured.
- League of Graphs and OP.GG pool-size statistics could not be found.

## 4. Mental and tilt management (stop-loss)

### Takeaway
The evidence is weak and mixed. A fan analysis (iTero) found about a 48.8% win rate after two straight losses. Gold players who keep queuing after 2 losses had the lowest win rate, but the pattern was different in Diamond. A PhD student's analysis found a small effect (about 2% more losses after a loss). Players commonly manage streaks by disengaging. A "stop after 2 losses" stop-loss is a reasonable consensus rule, not a proven one.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA-BLOG] iTero tilt article: a player who has just lost 2 games has "a 48.8% chance of winning their next game". The author says tilt "has not been proven". For Gold players, not taking a break after two straight losses had "by far the lowest win rate." For Diamond I, regular short breaks after a loss were linked to a considerable drop in win rate, and playing on immediately showed a small improvement. Samples are small and the baseline was adjusted. — [iTero: LoL tilt](https://itero.gg/articles/lol-tilt)
- [DATA-BLOG] A "loser queue" analysis by a PhD student found players lose about 2% more often after a loss and called it a low-significance discrepancy. — [devtrackers mirror](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/1e570d00-existence-of-loser-queue-a-statistical-analysis)
- [EVIDENCE] A CHI PLAY 2024 paper, "Streaks and Coping," analyzed 597,680 matches from top players to test whether streaks carry over. Only the abstract was visible. — [HKBU Scholars](https://scholars.hkbu.edu.hk/en/publications/streaks-and-coping-decoding-player-performance-in-league-of-legen/); [DOI via Unpaywall](https://unpaywall.org/10.1145%2F3665463.3678787)
- [EVIDENCE, qualitative] A 2018 CHI paper on perceived streakiness found that both winning and losing streaks hurt the player experience, and that disengagement was a primary coping strategy. — [Penn State Pure](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/playing-with-streakiness-in-online-games-how-players-perceive-and/)
- [DATA, old] Riot's 2013 internal data, reported by Engadget: raging players win 35% fewer matches than average, and the decline generally starts with one loss. This concerns toxicity, not tilt specifically. — [Engadget 2013](https://www.engadget.com/2013-09-12-raging-league-of-legends-players-win-35-fewer-matches-than-aver.html)
- [OPINION, unverified] LoLTheory blog argues for a "stop after 2 losses" rule and cites a 2024 Frontiers in Psychology finding of about a 30-minute tilt window. I could not verify that study. The blog reads as promotional, so do not cite the study claim. — [LoLTheory blog](https://blog.loltheory.gg/cant-end-on-a-loss-league/)
- [DATA, secondary] Chat restrictions for toxic players reportedly improved teamwork and win rates. No figures were checkable. — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/league-of-legends-restricting-chat-for-rude-players-leads-to-better-teamwork/amp)

### Inferences
- A practical stop-loss for a Platinum player: stop or take a real break after 2 straight losses, or after one loss that made you angry. This matches the iTero Gold-tier pattern and the coping literature, but its effect size is small and depends on rank.
- Muting chat is low-cost and fits the Riot toxicity data. Recommend it as the default for players who tilt easily.

### Gaps
- I found no peer-reviewed study showing that stopping after N losses raises LoL win rate.
- I could not locate or verify the 2024 Frontiers "30-minute tilt" study.

## 5. Solo queue communication (pings, timers)

### Takeaway
I found no data linking in-game ping use to LoL solo queue win rate. The academic ping research is on Heroes of the Storm, or is qualitative LoL work without win rates. Communication advice (ping intentions, timers, Flash cooldowns) is opinion-based.

### Cited Findings
- [EVIDENCE, other game] "Ping to win?" (CHI 2016) studied non-verbal pings and team performance in Heroes of the Storm. A 2023 ACM paper on pings and team performance also used Heroes of the Storm. — [ACM via Unpaywall](https://unpaywall.org/10.1145%2F3557022)
- [EVIDENCE, qualitative LoL] A 2023 Brazilian study of 12 matches from six streamers found older players often underuse the ping system. A University of North Dakota thesis found players combine basic pings to express more complex ideas. Neither measured win rate. — [UFRGS LUME](https://lume.ufrgs.br/handle/10183/272504); [UND thesis](https://commons.und.edu/theses/4276)
- [DATA, network latency, not pings] A Riot writer (Christokkies) analyzed 95M+ games and found win probability barely changed with latency gaps for many champions. This is about network ping, not communication. — [Millenium](https://www.millenium.org/news/218863.html)
- [OPINION] Tracking guides list ignoring your own lanes' information and forgetting enemy summoner spell status as common errors. A flashless or low-HP target changes the enemy jungler's priorities. — [Disciplined Jungler](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tracking/)
- [OPINION] Ward the enemy jungler's likely path ahead of time, "not only after they could already be there." — [Boosteria (Wild Rift)](https://boosteria.org/guides/wild-rift-jungle-tracking-basics-pathing-vision-calls)

### Inferences
- Recommended solo queue protocol (opinion): ping the enemy jungler's predicted location when it is likely, around 3:00–3:30. Ping "on my way" 5–10 s before a gank. Type objective timers and enemy Flash timers in chat. Use "enemy missing" plus a question ping on the side where the enemy jungler was last seen.

### Gaps
- I found no quantitative evidence on ping frequency vs win rate in LoL.

## 6. Catalog of common Platinum jungle mistakes and fixes

### Takeaway
The mistakes that appear across sources are: farming without ganking (or the reverse), pathing to camps instead of opportunities, forcing objectives without lane priority, only thinking about the enemy jungler when you can see them, ignoring summoner spells and lane state, warding late or never buying Control Wards, playing complex mechanical champions, and itemizing without regard to the enemy team. The fixes are mostly coaching opinion.

### Cited Findings (mistake → fix)
1. **Gank/farm imbalance** ("barely ever have a balance between ganking and farming") → plan a gank window after the first clear, based on lane state. — [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-how-to-climb-low-elo-jungler/)
2. **Pathing to camps instead of opportunities** → choose the route toward lanes that can be ganked or have priority. — [r/summonerschool](https://r.datuan.dev/r/summonerschool)
3. **Forcing objectives without lane priority** → push lanes or confirm priority before starting dragon or grubs. — [r/summonerschool](https://r.datuan.dev/r/summonerschool); [나무위키 정글 (카정 requires lane priority/smite/vision knowledge)](https://namu.wiki/w/%EC%A0%95%EA%B8%80(%EB%A6%AC%EA%B7%B8%20%EC%98%A4%EB%B8%8C%20%EB%A0%88%EC%A0%84%EB%93%9C))
4. **Only thinking about the enemy jungler when they're on screen** → keep a running prediction from their start, clear speed and CS. — [Disciplined Jungler](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tracking/)
5. **Forgetting summoner spell status** → track Flash timers, since a flashless lane is a gank target for both junglers. — [Disciplined Jungler](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tracking/)
6. **Ignoring your own lanes' information** (enemy laners backing or roaming) → check the minimap and your laners' screens. — [Disciplined Jungler](https://disciplinedjungler.com/guides/jungle-tracking/)
7. **Reactive or no warding; no Control Wards** → ward ahead of the predicted path and buy Control Wards. — [Boosteria](https://boosteria.org/guides/wild-rift-jungle-tracking-basics-pathing-vision-calls); [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/?p=22141)
8. **Overly complex champions** (Lee Sin, Nidalee) → play simpler, consistent junglers. — [Mobalytics](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-how-to-climb-low-elo-jungler/)
9. **Wide champion pool / low mastery** → 2–3 champions (about 44% WR under ~20 games on a champion). — [iTero mastery](https://www.itero.gg/articles/mastery-a-statistical-summary); [OP.GG 심해탈출](https://talk.op.gg/s/lol/user-news/209224/)
10. **Items that ignore the enemy team** ("상대픽을 보고 맞춰서 들어야한다", build according to the enemy picks) → adapt your build to enemy threats. — [Mobalytics 7 common mistakes](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-7-common-mistakes/); [Inven 팁](http://www.inven.co.kr/board/lol/2766/33930)
11. **No plan after backing** → plan the next route and predict the enemy jungler while in base (Korean Platinum-escape tip). — [Inven 팁](http://www.inven.co.kr/board/lol/2766/33930)
12. **Not reviewing losses** → review 3–5 losses where the enemy jungler outplayed you. — [FMKorea](https://www.fmkorea.com/260370499)

### Inferences
- A suggested order for fixing these at Platinum, based on how often sources mention them and how much impact they have: #2/#3 (decision-making) → #4/#5/#6 (tracking) → #9/#8 (champion pool) → #7 (vision) → #10/#11.

### Gaps
- I found no data source that measures how often each mistake occurs at Platinum. Mobalytics GPI and League of Graphs breakdowns by tier were not accessible.
- Most Korean sources found are from 2014–2017. I could not reach any 2022–2026 Korean Platinum jungle guides with full content.
- I found no Naver Blog posts. Search returned Inven, OP.GG Talk, FMKorea, Namu Wiki and ygosu ([ygosu 상대 정글 동선 예측](https://m.ygosu.com/board/lo_legend/130943/)).
